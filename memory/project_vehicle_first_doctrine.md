**CHASE'S VEHICLE-FIRST DOCTRINE (2026-07-10, verbatim intent — governs the #87 agentic audit + ALL OTTO-Q energy/assignment logic from now on):**

1. **Chargers/vehicles are NEVER held back from service.** Free charger + vehicle needing charge → route IMMEDIATELY, "regardless of energy tariff or anything else." Vehicle needs ALWAYS come first: charging, cleaning, potential service, parking/staging.
2. **Energy optimization comes STRICTLY from analysis (forecasting) + BESS usage.** When OTTO-Q foresees dozens of arrivals in the next hour/period, it identifies this AHEAD OF TIME and pre-emptively draws from the big on-site battery instead of hitting grid peak. "Vehicles charge and the chargers are utilized regardless — we optimize depot flow and energy usage AROUND this."
   - **CONSEQUENCE:** the EN.001 grid-capacity-ceiling overrides that deferred stall assignments (seen live: 82 vehicles stuck at gate while 8/10 DCFC empty at 2pm peak) VIOLATE this doctrine and must be re-architected. The −45.6%/$276k A/B headline was partly built on charge deferral → **must re-certify honestly under the new doctrine** (BESS + timing only). OPEN QUESTION for Chase: does modulating charge RATE (slower kW while still charging) count as "holding back"? Strict read = yes, disallowed.
3. **Twin must generate + COMMUNICATE per-vehicle needs** (cleaning, service, staging requirements — not just charging) as run variability, fed to OTTO-Q; OTTO-Q returns an optimized depot flow + service flow accommodating them. **The 5 canonical scenarios (must all be expressible + handled):**
   1. charge → interior clean → immediate dispatch
   2. charge → service → staging → cleaning → quick charge → dispatch
   3. charge → staging overnight
   4. charge → immediate dispatch
   5. immediate service → staging overnight or until TECHNICIAN GREEN-LIGHT for dispatch
4. **Global awareness:** OTTO-Q must simultaneously know (a) each vehicle's needs, (b) energy needs/usage, (c) depot utilization + features, (d) EVERY other vehicle's sequence at any given time — not greedy per-vehicle decisions (the 72% occupied-stall-proposal override rate is exactly this failure).

6. **Twin motion still bad** per Chase (likely viewed pre-merge — gridlock fixes merged 2026-07-10; verify on deployed Lovable after Publish) — bounce back to it between OTTO-Q rounds, do not lose.
