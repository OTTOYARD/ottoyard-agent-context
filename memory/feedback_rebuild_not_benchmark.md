# ⭐ STANDING: WE ARE REBUILDING, NOT BENCHMARKING

> *"I don't care about published numbers or percentages or anything. We are rebuilding and
> correcting, not worried about beating a baseline or anything like that until everything 'works'
> and 'moves' correctly."*
> — Chase, 2026-08-06

**Why:** the numbers rest on foundations that are actively being replaced. The depot layout was wrong
(54 overlapping stalls, 20 of 45 chargers unreachable), the completion ledger was crediting services
that never happened (11 of 16 credits fabricated), and the ETA was a constant graded against itself.
Optimising a percentage computed on top of those is measuring noise. Chase already knows this — he
does not need it re-explained every time a number moves.

## HOW TO APPLY
- **Do NOT** lead reports with "+85.8% throughput" / "utilisation falls 6.3%" / "must re-certify the
  −41% headline." Do not build re-certification into the plan as a gate during rebuild.
- **DO** lead with: does it work, does it move correctly, is the record honest.
- **Still state a number when it is EVIDENCE of correctness**, not when it is a score:
  "0 over-credits of 6 exits" ✅ · "throughput improved 12%" ❌ (during rebuild).
- **Still flag when a change makes a prior claim VOID** — one line, no re-cert plan attached. He needs
  to know not to quote a stale figure externally; he does not need a certification schedule.
- Direction-of-effect is fine and useful ("the lot doubled so travel legs get longer"). Precision
  percentages on top of a broken foundation are not.
- ⚠️ This is a REBUILD-PHASE instruction. When the foundation is sound and Chase is preparing external
  material, comparative numbers matter again — see [[reference_ottoq_real_edge]] and
  [[project_ecosystem_positioning]].
