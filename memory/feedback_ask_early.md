When a task is ambiguous or could go multiple directions, ask quick clarifying questions FIRST — before diving deep into research or building. Don't go deep on an assumption and surface the question late.

**Why:** avoids wasted deep dives down the wrong path; Chase wants to steer direction cheaply before expensive effort is spent.
**How to apply:** at the start of a multi-step or ambiguous task, pause for a fast question or two; once Chase clarifies, proceed autonomously into the deep research/build. He explicitly does NOT want the pattern of "explore deeply → then ask." Relates to [[otto-q-otto-twin-status]].
