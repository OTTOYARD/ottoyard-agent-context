**Rules this sets (supersede any earlier partial-charge/deploy-ready-80% thinking — that lever is RETIRED):**
1. **Charge target = ~100% (99-100%), always.** Never deploy-ready-partial. The 2026-07-24 "deploy-ready 80% + yield" CapEx lever is VOID — do not quote it.
2. **A visit is ATOMIC:** full charge + EVERY required service completed before redeploy. Deploy gate must become `soc >= ~99 AND zero open needs` (today it is `soc>=80 AND no open MUST-DO needs` — needs upgrading).

- **DCFC sessions per stall per hour: 0.78 if charging to 100% — but 1.79 if you hand off at 80%.** Chase's "2-3 sessions/hr/stall" is achievable ONLY with the handoff (and rises toward it for higher arrival SoC). Charging to 100% on a DCFC wastes the asset: **the last 20% of charge takes MORE stall-time than the first 55%, and no amount of charger power fixes it — the battery simply won't accept fast charge up there.**
- **THE STRATEGY: DCFC to ~80% → hand off to L2 for the top.** For 100 vehicles / 7h window: **DCFC-only = 22 DCFC, 3,300 kW peak, ~$2.2M charger CapEx** vs **handoff = 10 DCFC + 19 L2, 1,865 kW peak, ~$1.11M** → **~$1.09M CapEx saved + ~1,435 kW less peak demand + 2.3× DCFC throughput.**
- **ANSWER TO "should we just buy more DCFC?": NO — past ~10 DCFC you are buying power the battery cannot absorb.** Marginal capacity is ~16× cheaper on L2 ($6k vs $100k/unit) and the taper work belongs there anyway. Rough optimal ratio ≈ **1 DCFC : 2 L2**. NUANCE for revenue: **size DCFC for the DAYTIME rapid-turnaround peak** (short 20→80 top-ups during high ride-hail demand = revenue-critical) and **size L2 for the OVERNIGHT full-charge volume** (cheap, slow, plenty of time). Two different jobs, two different sizing drivers.
- **Chase's L2 instinct was right, but for a stronger reason than thermal relief:** the taper is physics, so L2 is where the top-of-charge work *belongs*. Bonus: doing 80→100% slowly on AC is materially gentler on the pack — protects the fleet's most expensive asset. ⚠️ OPEN QUESTION: battery chemistry of target platforms (LFP tolerates daily 100%; NMC degrades faster) — affects whether daily-100% doctrine is asset-safe.
- **THE HANDOFF IS AN ORCHESTRATION PRODUCT FEATURE:** it needs a tech to unplug at 80% and move the vehicle to an L2 — which fits the zone-tech model exactly (the tech is already in the zone). OTTO-Q decides *which* vehicles hand off vs ride DCFC to full, based on deadline slack. **NOT YET IMPLEMENTED in brain/twin — this is now the #1 orchestration build.**
