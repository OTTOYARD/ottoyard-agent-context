**THE CORE PROBLEM:** vehicles move around the depot freely with NO notion of how long anything actually takes. There is no pacing tied to real service/stall durations, no inter-service wait, no drive time between stalls. They just float/swarm. That has to become a properly-timed sequence per vehicle, sequenced by OTTO-Q.

**(A) TIMING / SERVICE-DURATION LOGIC Chase dictated — each vehicle paced by REAL durations:**
- **L2 charging session ≈ 40 min** (Chase's stated demo number; reconcile w/ twin's service-time config + DCFC vs L2 split — DCFC is the fast one). Charging holds the vehicle in its charging stall for the FULL duration.
- **~30 sec–1 min delay BEFORE switching from one service to the next** (an inter-service settle/handoff pause).
- **~1 min to taxi/position from one service to the next** (e.g., drive from charging over to a wash bay).
- **Wash takes the long determined duration** (per [[../OTTOQ_SERVICE_OPS_RESEARCH]] cadences/durations).
- Then the vehicle is **stationed at the designated parking space OTTO-Q assigns** it.
- ALL of: arrival times, wait times between each step, and drive-time from one stall to another must be modeled + respected. Right now none of it is.

**(B) "NO LOOSE STAGING" — every vehicle is ALWAYS in exactly one of:** a charging stall / a parking stall / a washing bay / a service bay — OR taxiing between two of those. **There is NO "neatly stacked / stashed / loose staged" state.** (Chase pushed back hard on my "neat rows / staged" phrasing — a "staged" vehicle must be PARKED IN A CONCRETE ASSIGNED PARKING STALL, never a loose blob in a staging zone.)

**SPATIAL BINDING required:** tie in EACH parking stall, EACH charging space, the service bays, the washing bays, and the EXACT drivable lanes. Vehicles bound to concrete stall coords + the lane network end-to-end: realistic movement INTO the depot → around to each assigned stall → OUT of the depot.

**(B) MOTION REALISM still broken (the PR #14 separation/lag fix did NOT fully resolve it — Chase still sees sliding):** cars still **slide** and move rapidly/freely; they do NOT slow down, pause, and turn. Need real vehicle motion = **wheels turning, parking maneuvers (back out, pull forward), pulling into/out of stalls**, slow deliberate taxi — not gliding. **Wash bay must be OPEN with the vehicle visibly IN the bay.**
